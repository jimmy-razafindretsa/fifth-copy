# #114 Bot count, per-bot difficulty and public/private lobby toggle

Date: 2026-11-02. Outcome: built (UI, full). The host adds, re-levels and removes bots in the `FORM 5-L` BOTS row (one sub-row per bot) and switches the LOBBY row between PUBLIC and PRIVATE. Bots go through #110's `host:settings` patch; the lobby type is the new host-only action `setLobbyType`.

Decisions
- `FormKey` gains `bots` (ADD), `bots.${n}` (one sub-row, 1-based, `null` = remove) and `lobbyType`. `buildPatch` rebuilds the whole `bots` array; a new bot is Clerk (bible 14.3).
- `submitLobbyType` reuses the form state: its refusals map onto the room's (`closed` -> `not-waiting`, `not-found` -> `no-room`, `rate-limited` -> `timeout`), so no new copy.
- `Lobby.type` from the row wins over the room's copy in `LobbyLiveView` (ADR 0008). `revalidatePath` in the action refreshes the kicker and the prop; the socket stays connected.
- `set-lobby-type` limit: 30 per 60 s per host (ADR 0020 amendment).

Gotchas
- `lobby-components.test.tsx` must mock `../actions/set-lobby-type` (it pulls `env` and `db`), like `mint-race-token`.
- The five-level segmented group wraps at 768 in the `1fr` column, as DIFFICULTY does.
- The C2 e2e uses 4 patches in 10 s (add, add, level, remove), under the 5-per-10 s budget (#750).
