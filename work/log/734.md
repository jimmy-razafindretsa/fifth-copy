# #734 Lobby housekeeping: Postgres close on last leave and the expiry sweep job

2026-11-09. Outcome: a `Lobby` becomes CLOSED in Postgres when its room dies. The race server queues `POST /api/internal/lobbies/:id/close` (`PROTOCOL_VERSION` 12) in its results outbox when a leave closes a room and for each room `rehydrate` finds gone on boot; the web route (HMAC + nonce) writes CLOSED once. The worker job `lobby.expire` (every 15 min) closes WAITING lobbies older than 4 h (room TTL x 2) with no race started in that window.

Decisions: one outbox for results and closes (`sendOutboxEntry` dispatches on the close schema; outbox id `lobby:<lobbyId>`), so `drain` on boot resumes both. A 404 on a close is acknowledged (the lobby is gone; retrying could never succeed). A kept ended room ("Race again") is not closed by the leave; the sweep covers it once its keys expire. `closeLobbies` is the one CLOSED write (route and job). `ROOM_TTL_MS` and `openLobbyWhere` live in server-only-free modules so the worker bundle never imports `server-only`. ADR 0006 amendment proposed (route list).

Gotchas: the job sweeps the whole table; its DB test uses a 2020 clock so other test files' lobbies never match. Test user prefixes must not share a prefix with `lobby-model.test.ts`'s `test-lobby-` (its cleanup cascades to their lobbies).

Follow-ups: none.
