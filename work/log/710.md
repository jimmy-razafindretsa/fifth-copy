The one red run was a different flake: the limiter's Redis call took over 250 ms (`LIMIT_TIMEOUT_MS`), the limiter failed open, and the call answered `not-found`. Out of scope (limiter). - Gotcha: the noise loop needs a single committed-insert session (`DO ... LOOP ... COMMIT`); one psql per insert is too sparse to hit the window.
