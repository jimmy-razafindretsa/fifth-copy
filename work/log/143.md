# #143 Race again

2026-11-02. Outcome: after the Dawn report the same lobby races again. RACE AGAIN on the report leads back to `/lobby/[code]`. The room, still in `ended`, takes the host's START as a new race: new race id, fresh desks and traces, the same members, bots and settings. The previous results stay untouched.

Decisions
- `lifecycle.canStart(phase)` (`waiting | ended`) and an `onRestart` hook (ticker, desks runtime, idle warnings) are the two seams for the auto Race again (#128).
- `registry.startRace` is the one reset: it drops `endReason`/`endedAt`, the desks mirror and the trace lists of the old and new desks in its MULTI.
- When the last human leaves an `ended` room, the room is kept under `ENDED_GRACE_TTL_S` (600 s, keys.ts) and returns `kept`. The process frees it as a close does (#658). The next join refreshes the TTL to `ROOM_TTL_S`.
- Admission (`mayRejoin`, handshake and join): in `ended` only, the host and the last race's racers come back. A racer gets its old desk when that desk is free. Nobody else gets in (late joiners are #732).
- No wire change. PROTOCOL_VERSION stays 7.

Gotchas
- A voided room (`ended`, `endReason: void`) can now race again too (rehydrate.test updated).
- Once the grace TTL passes, the report still shows RACE AGAIN while `Lobby` is WAITING. The lobby page then shows its existing "no room" line. #734 owns the close.
- The stamp CSS is copied from the lobby module, because cross-feature CSS imports are forbidden.

Follow-ups: none.
