# #658 Race server: members who dropped during a race are released at race end

Date: 2026-10-23. Outcome: delivered (PR pending). After a non-void race ends, every member presence seated through a socket that now has none leaves the room through the same path as a disconnect (`players/leave.ts`: `leaveRoom`, `releaseDropped`), so the last human leaving closes the room and `onClosed` frees the desks, presence, idle and ticker state.

## Decisions
- No ADR. Inside ADR 0008's room model.
- Void races keep their members: they reconnect to read `ended { void }` (#204).
- Only users seated through a socket on this process are released (`presence.dropped`); members written straight into the registry (rehydrate tests) are left alone.

## Gotchas
- `releaseDropped` is started from `onRaceEnded`, inside the ending's room queue, and not awaited: its own `withRoom` runs after the ending.
