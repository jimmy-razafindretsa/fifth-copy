Decisions
- `settings-form.ts` is pure. `buildPatch` is the one place a control becomes a patch, and #114 adds `bots` there. `validate` is `raceSettingsPatchSchema.safeParse`. The bounds are read from `raceSettingsSchema` (`minValue`/`maxValue`, and the letters maximum from the schema's own `too_big` issue).
- CUSTOM starts from NORMAL's middle recipe (medium, common letters, light), recorded in bible 14.3.
- While a change is pending, controls are `aria-disabled` and inputs `readOnly`, not `disabled`, so keyboard focus stays put. Only the minutes under NO TIMER use `disabled`.
- On a refusal a `revision` counter remounts the uncontrolled inputs, so they show the broadcast value again.
