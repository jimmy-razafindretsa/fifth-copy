# #46 One-time recovery code: reveal at sign-up and /recover

- Word list: 2048 words (1024 EN + 1024 FR concrete nouns, hand-curated, isClean), `src/features/identity/recovery/wordlist.ts`. 10 words drawn with `crypto.getRandomValues` (11-bit mask, 110 bits drawn). The hash covers the sorted canonical form (order, case, accents ignored), so ~88 bits are effective against guessing; online guessing is capped by the limiter.
- One-shot reveal: sign-up seals the words into `fc_recovery` (A256GCM via jose, key = sha256 of a domain tag + AUTH_SECRET; HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict, path `/sign-up/recovery`, 10 min). The page opens it for the signed-in owner and claims its `jti` in Redis (`SET NX`); a reload shows ALREADY SHOWN. Redis down: fails open (shows again) rather than lose the account.
- `/recover`: client 30/h then account 5/h budgets (`recovery-redeem`, `recovery-redeem-account`), one Argon2id compare on every path (DUMMY_HASH), guarded rotation, `tokenVersion + 1`, new words returned once. User is not signed in.
- Bible 14.3 extended with "Recovery code" (FORM 1-D / 1-E).
- Discovered (not fixed): `/sign-in` has no link to `/recover` (bible says no forgot-password path; owner to decide whether `/recover` gets a link).
