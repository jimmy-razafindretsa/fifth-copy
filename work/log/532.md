# #532 CI e2e job fits its time budget (Playwright shards)

Date: 2026-10-06. Outcome: PR #542 reviewed and QA'd by Deliver; C2, C3 ticked, C1 pending its on-main verify after merge.

## Decisions
- No ADR (governing 0003, 0004). `e2e` became a 4-way `--shard` matrix job `e2e-shard` (fail-fast off, 15 min timeout) plus an aggregating job named `e2e` (`needs: e2e-shard`, `if: always()`, passes only when `needs.e2e-shard.result == success`). The required check name, `.github/branch-protection.json` and `scripts/merge.sh` are unchanged.

## Evidence
- PR run 37497720355 (head 3c24e67): shards 3m33s, 3m10s, 7m39s, 6m45s; gate `e2e` success; whole e2e stage about 8.5 min (it was 14-18 min, and timed out at 20).
- `--list`: 249 tests, same as main (the branch only touches the three CI/test files).

## Gotchas
- Shards are uneven (shards 3 and 4 hold the race specs). If a shard nears 12 min, raise `shardTotal`; the tests require the matrix to stay 1..N.
- Matrix jobs are not required contexts. The protection test filters them out.
