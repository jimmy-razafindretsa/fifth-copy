# #927 3D room adapts to small lobbies

Date: 2026-11-12. Outcome: built (UI builder), PR open; Deliver pending.

## Decisions
- No ADR (0013, 0017 hold): the layout lives inside the seat embed, which alone knows the frame's aspect. No wire change, nothing under `src/`, `packages/`, `services/`.
- One pure block in `docs/design/embeds/Fifth Copy Seat.html` between `// seating:start` and `// seating:end` (`povLayout`, `overLayout`, `pitch`), unit-tested from the served page's source. `arrange()` applies it to stations, clerks, camera and the `<html>` report (`data-seats|layout|fov|ring|rivals-in-view`).
- The 30 stations left the merged room mesh for one `BatchedMesh` (instance = ring slot). Clerk identity per slot unchanged (`(desk - self) mod 30`); only transforms move.
- Bible 11.1 and 11.3 record the two extensions of the reference: the ring-radius rule and the odd-count overview offset `π/(2n)`.

## Gotchas
- The grime shader must apply `batchingMatrix` (`#ifdef USE_BATCHING`), or every batched desk samples the grime at the origin.
- `arrange()` runs on every `fc-race-state` (progress ticks): it is cheap and resets the overview orbit only when the view or the player count changes.
- Seat embed weight 215.3 of 220 kB gzip: little room left.
