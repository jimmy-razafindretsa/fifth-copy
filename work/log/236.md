# #236 Sabotage tray: docket, effects on targets, cooldown and immunity feedback

Date: 2026-11-03. Outcome: built (ui), PR open. The tray is live: the card from `bonus-earned`, `ENTER TO PLAY` plus a `PLAY CARD` button, cooldown seconds once a second, the `IMMUNE` row; targets get the overlay (Extra Paperwork, Exemption) and the Smoke Break blur.

## Decisions
- No wire change (protocol v7) and no ADR. The client sets `cooldownUntil` itself from the engine's `COOLDOWN_MS`, re-exported through `client/prediction.ts`, the race feature's one engine door. `bonus-sent` confirms it and a refusal rolls it back.
- The play is a store event (`bonus-play`). The reducer gates it (card, typing, not cooling, no play in flight) and `sendBonusPlays` emits one `bonus:play` per accepted play. Enter and the button take the same path.
- A new `countdown` clears the overlay. Before, a waiting-room overlay could carry into the next race, which `counters.test.ts` relied on; it now sends a `bonus-hit`.
- Bible 7.4 and 7.7 extended: the refused row, the button, and the blur on the tape and typed lines.

## Gotchas
- Exemption's band is [0.67, 0.75), and 3rd of 4 is 0.667 (Extra Paperwork). The smallest field that deals it is 6th of 8 (`e2e/race/bonus.spec.ts`).
- On the wire, `no-bonus` also means "no target" (everyone ahead finished). The tray shows `IMMUNE` for that case too.
