# #240 Race timer and finished waiting view

Date: 2026-10-31. Outcome: delivered (PR #722). Deliver verified C1-C5 itself. `counters.test` passed (15). `store.test` passed (71). `RACE_FAST_CLOCK=1 finish.spec` passed twice, plus once on the normal clock. `npm run typecheck` passed. `check.sh` was all green after one re-run of the known flaky `results route.test`.

## Decisions
- No ADR. Follows 0006, 0007, 0010, 0013. There is one reason-agnostic `ended` reducer case (`ending`, `endedAt`); the view switches on the reason, and `void` renders nothing so it stays free for #661. The final docket renders `ended.ranking` exactly as the server sent it.
- Bible extensions: 7.11 "Timer tube" (with three tubes the bezel goes compact, the numerals sized by a container query on the nixie slot) and 14.3 "Finish and race over" (finish stamps, FILED and RACE OVER dockets, DAWN REPORT ink link). `.ink` moved, unchanged, into `hud-docket.module.css`.

## Gotchas
- `RACE_FAST_CLOCK=1` never scales `timerS` (minimum 60 s) but cuts the idle kick to about 6 s. A timer e2e must keep every desk awake, or the race ends early as `all-finished`.
- `useRaceStore` reads `initialRaceState` during SSR, so `renderToStaticMarkup` of a store leaf shows the before-start seat. Mock the hook to test `SeatFinish`-style glue.

## Follow-ups (pre-existing, not caused by this card)

- When every desk is asleep or a timer ends a typing desk, the teleprinter keys still read enabled. #224 disables them for a finished desk only; the input itself is disabled.
