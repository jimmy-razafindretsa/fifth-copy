# #25 Printed poster texture and constructivist motifs

- Date: 2026-10-07
- Outcome: five opt-in `@utility` blocks in `docs/design/tokens.css` (`paper-grain`, `paper-grain-red`, `sun-rays`, `ink-misregister`, `device-phosphor` / `device-nixie`). The device glow is scoped with `&:where([data-device], [data-device] *)`. The textures paint nothing under `prefers-contrast: more`. Two server primitives: `Band` (`clampAngle` 30-45, default 38) and `Device` (bezel, `data-device`). `/design` gains a "Printed motifs" section. Bible 6 gains rows for the steep band, misregistration and device glow, plus an opt-in / contrast-more line.
- Deliver verdict: pass, 8 of 8 ticked. CI is green on d04e5c8, including the visual job. Local `visual-baselines --check` is still broken by #544.
- Decisions: no ADR. Grains and sun rays are background layers on the host element, as in the hero recipe, not pseudo-elements. They cannot take hit tests, and that meets the C1/C4 `pointer-events: none` intent. Setting `pointer-events: none` on the host would also disable its content. "READY" in the phosphor `Device` uses `type-label`, because bible 4 keeps VT323 for numerals only.
- Gotchas: Tailwind 4 `@utility` accepts both a nested `&:where(...)` and a nested `@media` block, so no `@layer` fallback was needed. A `Band` is full-bleed only inside its own box: the caller sizes that box, and the box clips the 300% strip.
- Follow-ups: #610 (sun-ray stages to the `sun-rays` utility).
