# #967 Personnel file: Ta place au Ministère (typist census)

2026-11-17. `/profile` shows `RECENSEMENT DES DACTYLOS` / `Ta place au Ministère` after the clean streaks and before the output curve, from 3 counted races and only when `getCensus` (#966, ADR 0023) returns a census (30 typists or more). Tabs speed/accuracy, toggle average/best; the sentence, 60 bars, the cumulative curve, the `TOI` marker and the ticks redraw on the client from the four distributions sent whole.

Decisions: pure geometry in `src/features/stats/view/census-plot.ts` (`CENSUS_MODES`, `CENSUS_METRICS`, `CENSUS_KEYS`: a fifth distribution is one more entry); client leaf `components/typist-census.tsx` (`CensusSection` stateless for tests); server slot `components/census-slot.tsx` awaits the read inside `ProfileScreen`'s own `Suspense` and renders nothing on `null` or a rejected read. `/design#profile` two-week specimens pass the `middle` (guest) and `top` (account, tag flipped left) fixtures. Violet curve added to bible 3.1 and components.md; deviations in bible 14.3.

Gotchas: SVG children of a `role="img"` div still surface as `img` for Playwright: `aria-hidden` on the SVG. The e2e database never reaches 30 typists, so the e2e checks absence on `/profile` (skipped if a shared DB ever holds 30) and the drawing on `/design`.

CI: `e2e/race/hud.spec.ts` C5 (Abandon 5 s cancel on `/design`) failed under load: `page.clock.install()` keeps flowing in real time, so the 200 ms margin before the 5 s timer was spent between steps (the heavier `/design` made it likelier). Fixed by pausing the installed clock before the timed steps; 8/8 at 4 workers locally.
