# #659 Lobby: a welcome during the countdown redirects to the race

Date: 2026-10-23 (build), 2026-10-24 (Deliver review + QA). Outcome: PR #665, Deliver verdict pass. `reduceLobby`'s `welcome` case now reads `room.phase` and `race`: a `countdown` or `running` welcome carrying the race gives `starting` with its `raceId` (the state a `countdown` broadcast gives), so `LobbyLive` redirects after a reload or a reconnect that missed the broadcast.

## Decisions
- No ADR. Follows 0006 (server-authoritative welcome).
- A `waiting` or `ended` welcome keeps the old `live` result; an `ended` welcome never redirects.
- A countdown/running welcome without `race` keeps a page that is already `starting` starting (START racing the join).

## Gotchas
- The race server builds `welcome.room.phase` from a fresh room read but `welcome.race` from the join result, so a welcome can say `countdown` with `race: null`. The reducer tolerates it; the `countdown` broadcast fills in the race.
