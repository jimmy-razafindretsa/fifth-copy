# #763 Podium C6 reduced-motion test waits for the mount queue

2026-11-03. Outcome: `e2e/results/podium.spec.ts` C6 takes its layout and podium `innerHTML` snapshots only once the plinth mount queue (#688 C4) has run dry: all three plinths visited (near-viewport latch), every frame `data-embed="mounted"` and its document `data-ready="1"`, then back to plinth 1. The "same layout, same markup" assertions after `data-motion="reduce"` are unchanged.

Decisions: wait for the queue rather than strip mount-state attributes from the comparison, so the markup check still covers the whole podium.

Gotchas: the queue keeps mounting plinths 2 and 3 for seconds after plinth 1 is ready (one `PLINTH_MOUNT_GAP_MS` per plinth plus the #690 settle); any whole-podium snapshot must wait for it. `frameStates` and `visitPlinths` (same spec) are the helpers. A first `--repeat-each 5` run on a cold `.next` cache with machine load ~18 on 4 cores failed 12/15 (output not kept); three later runs (two at scale 1, one at `E2E_TIMEOUT_SCALE=2`) passed 15/15.

Follow-ups: none.
