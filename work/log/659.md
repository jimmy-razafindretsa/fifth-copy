# #659 Lobby: a welcome during the countdown redirects to the race

Date: 2026-10-23. Outcome: delivered (PR #665). `reduceLobby`'s `welcome` case now reads `room.phase` and `race`: a `countdown` or `running` welcome carrying the race gives `starting` with its `raceId`, the state a `countdown` broadcast gives, so `LobbyLive` redirects after a reload or a reconnect that missed the broadcast.

## Decisions
- No ADR. Follows 0006 (server-authoritative welcome).
- A `waiting` or `ended` welcome keeps the old `live` result.
