# #884 Host reload keeps the host role

2026-11-11. A host's socket that drops in a `waiting` or `ended` room with another human seated now
goes through the same leave grace as the last human (`LAST_LEAVE_GRACE_MS`, 5 s):
`lastLeave.hostWithOthers` (services/race-server/src/players/last-leave.ts) replaces `hostOfEnded`,
and the socket server's disconnect handler defers the leave. Back within the grace (a reload): the
leave is dropped, nobody is promoted. Away past it: `leaveRoom` promotes as in #137.

Decisions: there is no explicit LEAVE socket event; the explicit leave of C3 is `leaveRoom` (no grace),
the path every non-disconnect leave takes. No wire change, `PROTOCOL_VERSION` unchanged.

Gotchas: a host who closes the page is now replaced after 5 s, not at once: the #137 C5 e2e waits the
grace plus 3 s. A public room's armed auto-start can still fire inside a deferred leave's grace (as for
the #734 last-human case).
