# #966 Typist census

2026-11-16. The stats feature can place a typist among all typists (average and best speed and accuracy) with aggregates only: `censusOf` (pure, `src/features/stats/view/census.ts`) and `getCensus` (`queries/get-census.ts`), exported from the stats index; fixtures for `/design` in `view/census-fixtures.ts`.

Decisions: ADR 0023 (proposed): typists = users with 3+ counted results (`countedPopulationWhere`), `null` under 30, Gaussian smoothing (4 WPM, 0.8 point), no count, id or other typist's figure; population memoised 10 minutes, viewer read fresh. Privacy page, inventory and PIA (flow, risk 20) name the use.

Gotchas: the population read (`readPopulation`) is private on purpose; tests reach it through `createCensusReader` with a stand-in or spied client. Percentile is floored to one decimal and capped at 99.9. The `/privacy` sentence changes its full-page visual baselines.

Follow-ups: #967 (the section).
