# #187 Spectator channel

Date: 2026-10-07. Outcome: Deliver pass, 6 of 6 contract items verified (PR #617). Pentest clean (2 minors, no advisory needed).

## Decisions
- No ADR: implements ADR 0006 point 8 and ARCHITECTURE 7.8 as written. Protocol v4 already had `role: spectator`, `welcome.you: null`, `rejected { spectator }`; no wire change.
- `roleFor(lobby, viewerId, { spectator })`: a spectator request wins over host (the host's projector tab is read-only).
- The spectator edge (`socket/spectator.ts`) branches in `server.ts` before presence, the registry and the race: whatever `sub` it carries, it cannot shadow, evict or line-cut a desk.
- Room names moved to the leaf `socket/rooms.ts` (`lobbyRoom`, `deskRoom`, `spectatorRoom`, `audience`) to avoid a `server.ts` <-> `spectator.ts` import cycle. Every room-wide emit goes to `audience`; desk events also go to `spectatorRoom`.
- `RoomState.code` added (read from the existing room hash) for the spectator `welcome`.

## Gotchas
- A spectator joining a voided room (#204) also gets `ended { void }`, like a player. Not in the contract, harmless.
- The spectator `welcome` carries `room.code`. Fine today because minting needs the code; #292, #296 and #143 must not hand a spectator token to someone who does not hold the code.

## Follow-ups
- #616 / #207: no cap on spectator sockets per room or IP.
- #600: `check.sh` was green here; presence and identity suites also green alone.
