# #864 Personal card: your headline figures and bonuses on the Dawn report

2026-11-10. `getDawnReport` now carries `personal` (`src/features/results/view/personal.ts`): the
viewer's own place, clean WPM, accuracy, status, suspicious flag and bonuses sent/received by kind,
`null` for a spectator or visitor. The `PersonalCard` (7.4 docket on newsprint, `FORM 5-R · YOUR COPY`)
fills the `personal` slot beside the ranking from 1024px, stacked under it below.

Decisions: bonus tallies follow the engine ledger (`record`): a play counts once whatever its targets,
a self-exemption is sent, not received (tested against `record`). Only counts leave the query, never a
log. A spectator's ranking spans the full row (`.slots:has(> [data-slot="personal"])`). Bible 14.4
"Personal stats card" added. `ReportStatus` moved to `view/report-status.ts` (type cycle).

Gotchas: the six `/design` full-page baselines change (`#results-personal` specimens). Sibling cards
(graph, keys, tip, #321 best flag) add fields to `PersonalStats` and sections after the bonus rows.
