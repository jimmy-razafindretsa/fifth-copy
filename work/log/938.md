# #938 Clerks have no pupils on the Low graphics preset

2026-11-13. Every clerk the seat scene draws now shows its pupils on every preset; on LOW, 29 of 29 (POV) and 30 of 30 (overview) instead of 5 of 29.

- Cause: `Clerks.updateLod` hid the whole `eyes` part (pupils and glints) of every clerk past the preset's nearest LOD0 seats (LOW 6, MEDIUM 12); the eye whites belong to the head mesh, so far clerks stared blank. Not sub-pixel pupils, not the lenses.
- Fix (embed source, regenerated): the eyes part gets an LOD1 geometry (pupils at half segments, no glints); at LOD1 it follows the head with no glance or blink. It stays in the clerks' one `BatchedMesh`, so draw calls are unchanged (19 on LOW, 6 or 30 clerks). Bible 10.1 LOD1 budget line updated.
- Test hooks on `<html>`: `data-pupils` (drawn clerks with pupils / drawn clerks) and `data-calls` (draw calls per frame, every second); spec `e2e/race/scene-pupils.spec.ts` (`@scene`).
- Gotcha: LOD ranking counts hidden (empty-desk) clerks, so in a small staged room the rivals can all sit at LOD1 on LOW (a room of 6 reported 0 of 5 pupils before the fix). Out of scope here; follow-up #940.
