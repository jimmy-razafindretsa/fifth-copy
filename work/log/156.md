# #156 Desk identity allocator for humans and bots (bot seats)

Date: 2026-10-07. Outcome: delivered (PR #606). Deliver verified C1-C5: `bots.test.ts` 14 passed, with `lifecycle.test.ts` 33 passed, rooms + socket 171 passed, boundaries and typecheck:workspaces green, PR CI green.

## Decisions
- No ADR. Follows 0005, 0006 (bots are server-side members), 0008 (bot records in the members hash, TTL in the same MULTI).
- Bots never take desk 1: the room opens (and seats its bots) before the host joins, so desk 1 is kept for the first human. This is how C1 ("human joined first at 1, bots at 2 and 3") holds; the contract is unchanged.
- The last human leaving closes the room even if bots remain (ARCHITECTURE 7.1 "last human leaves"). Without this, a room with only bots left stays open until the TTL and `onRoomClosed` never runs.
- Bot ids are `bot:<desk>`, kept out of the wire (`userId: null` in the start request). `join` refuses a `bot:` sub.

## Gotchas
- The TTL checks in `bots.test.ts` run after a human join, which refreshes the TTL anyway, so they do not catch a missing TTL on the bot writes alone. Deliver checked it with a separate probe: open, setBots and a bots patch each set the TTL.
- An untimed race with a bot ends only at `MAX_RACE_MS` until #366 makes bots type.
- `check.sh` unit: `rename-user` and the `isClean` perf test failed under load. Both pass when run alone (#600).
