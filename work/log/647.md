# #647 Load-tolerant tests: isClean performance and not-found C4 on mobile

Date: 2026-10-30. Outcome: delivered (PR #709). Next.js 16.3.8, 4-core box.

## Decisions
- C1: `isClean` performance asserts the minimum of 20 timed batches against the unchanged 200 ms bound (#600 pattern); the file joins vitest `SLOW_FILES`.
- C2: a plain wait could not fix C4. A server action posted on a 404 document is answered 404 `text/x-component` and Next then reloads the page itself (a main-frame navigation on every FR click); that reload can abort the action body with no further click. `e2e/fixtures.ts` tolerates exactly that (`isNotFoundActionAbort`: `ERR_ABORTED`, `next-action` POST, 404 Flight response, path whose document answered 404); C4 settles each action before the next step.

## Deliver evidence
- Production build, `CI=1 --retries=0`, mobile C4: main's tests 4 of 40 failed (`requestfailed: POST /no-such-desk net::ERR_ABORTED`); branch 80 of 80 passed. FR-only probe: 30 of 30 reloads, 1 of 30 aborted the POST with no EN click.
- Mutant (not committed): idle minMs here ~22 ms, so 10x passes (175-185 ms); 12x (200-233 ms) and 15x fail. The contract's "idle minimum exceeds 200 ms" is what fails the test; the multiplier depends on the box.

## Gotchas
- An orphan `next-server` on the card's `PW_PORT` is silently reused by Playwright outside `CI`: check the port before a QA run.
- All not-found specs x3 viewports x10 (prod): 1 unidentified failure in 1530 runs (artifacts lost); watch for it.
