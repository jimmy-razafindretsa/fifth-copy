## Decisions
- No ADR. Follows 0007 (pure engine, seeded `mulberry32`, no `Math.random`/`Date.now`), 0006, 0008. No protocol change, no `ENGINE_VERSION` bump.
- `analyseTrace(input, thresholds)`: the card's `(keys, text, settings)` cannot express `unreproducible` or `timing-anomalies`.
- `MIN_ANOMALY_RATIO_KEYS = 50` accepted: anomalies count batches, so the 5 % ratio on a short trace would flag one Wi-Fi stall; the absolute cap of 20 always applies.
- `scripted-constant` is 153 chars, so the fixed 10 s window measures 183.6 WPM and only `regular-rhythm` fires. A 50 ms rhythm over a long text yields `wpm-cap` and `regular-rhythm` (checked by hand).

## Gotchas
- The race-server test reads engine fixtures with `readFileSync` (data, not an import).
- The DB route tests can time out in `beforeAll` on a cold Postgres; rerun.
- When per-player text overlays land (`effectiveText`), the replay must use the desk's effective text, or innocent desks get `unreproducible`.
