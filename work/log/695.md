Date: 2026-10-30. Outcome: delivered (PR #702) after two Deliver fails on C4 (a) and two replans (owner DECISIONs of 2026-10-30, the second "Benchmark avant la salle", contract hash f61c023fd1fe). ## Decisions
- No new ADR. ADR 0017 gains an Amendments line (frame budget, probe before the room, `data-bench`/`data-room`, the sliced build, `?scene=pin`); it stays consistent with ARCHITECTURE.md (the embed delivery and the `fc-scene-*` messages are unchanged; three.js stays out of every Next chunk and is loaded by the embed from `/3d/vendor/`).
- The `auto` benchmark is a fixed WebGL2 probe (128 blended full-buffer quads in the clear colour at the LOW buffer), drawn before three.js loads. Below `PROBE_MIN_FPS` (13) the page goes to paper and the room is never built. `PROBE_MEDIUM_FPS` 53 and `PROBE_HIGH_FPS` 57 were calibrated on SwiftShader only (table in bible 12).
- The frame budget (`src/features/race-3d/frame-budget.ts`, 18 fps, 3 strikes) is applied in `RaceScene`, not `SeatScene`, so `frameBudget` stays behind the lazy race-3d boundary.
- `scripts/embeds.ts` follows a dynamic `import('three')` too, so the seat's 220 kB budget still counts three.js (measured 213 kB).

## Gotchas
- A canvas at `opacity: 0` is not rasterized by Chromium, so a benchmark on it reads a false high fps. The scene layer is never transparent or hidden while it is mounted.
- Under CDP CPU throttle 8, with 2 Playwright workers on a loaded 4-core box, C4 (a) passed 9 of 12 locally (scale 1: 4/6, scale 2: 5/6) and 6 of 6 with 1 worker. Two of the three misses were keys gaps (1585 and 1710 ms) after the scene was already gone, so the cause was page and harness cost under throttle. The third (1514 ms) came at GO, during a 1.4 s probe frame. The 1500 ms limits have little headroom on a shared runner.
- GitHub's "re-run failed jobs" copies the passing jobs of attempt 1 into attempt 2: the shard 4/4 job of run 41078791892 ran only once.

## Follow-ups
- Real-GPU calibration of the probe, and the browser cap reported per telemetry second (#703, discovered). On a GPU the probe's 128x overdraw is limited by fill rate, unlike the room. Integrated laptop GPUs at 1080p may therefore read under 13 (paper) or in 27-33. A 27-33 reading is taken as a cap: MEDIUM, with the frame budget off for the whole session, because telemetry `capped` is fixed at the probe.
