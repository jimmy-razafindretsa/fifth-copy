# #732 Late joiners: race-in-progress notice, spectator hold, seat at the end

2026-11-03. A viewer refused `in-progress` before its first seat is held (store phase `spectating`, the 7.4 notice row over skeleton rows, no START) and seated when the race ends, without a reload.

Decisions
- Race server: `mayRejoin` admits anyone in `ended`, as in `waiting` (spec 5.1); countdown and running stay members only. A newcomer in `ended` skips the last race's desks, so a returning racer keeps its desk. Wire unchanged (PROTOCOL_VERSION 7).
- `late-join.ts` `joinRoom` owns the whole connection (first player join included), so the page has one code path; the spectator socket never reaches the store. `LATE_JOIN_RETRY_MS` = 10 s; `room-full` polls the player join only (about 6 mints a minute, under the 10/min limit).
- `in-progress` enters the hold while `you === null` (loading, or a first connect that went through reconnecting); a viewer seated before keeps the generic error (C4).

- Fix cycle 1: the room seats at most `ROOM_SEATS` (= `MAX_BOTS + 1`) typists, in `registry.join` and the handshake (`room-full`; members and bots unaffected). A server disconnect before the first welcome (`RoomSocket.onServerDisconnect`) is held like `in-progress`.

Gotchas
