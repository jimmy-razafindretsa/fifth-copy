## Gotchas
- C5 is a wall-clock test (200 ms). On a host at load average 50-100 it fails locally (361-650 ms), and so do unrelated timeouts (`scripts/deploy-smoke.test.ts`). Judge it on CI or on a quiet host. Do not loosen it.
- `1` and `l` share `i`: short names that differ from an entry only by i/l are blocked.
- Non-Latin letters are dropped, not mapped: Cyrillic/Greek homoglyphs are a bypass path for the #36 pentest.
- Repeated-x names are blocked (whole entry `xxx`, longer runs match by design).
