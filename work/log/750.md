# #750 Size the host:settings rate budget for the host settings form

Date: 2026-11-03. Outcome: built (Builder), awaiting Deliver.

## Decisions
- ADR 0020 amendment (#750): `host:settings` is 30 per 10 s per token identity (was 5). The form (#110) sends one patch per control (13 controls, up to 2 per bot row, #114) with one patch in flight; `setHostSettings` in e2e spends the same budget. 30 keeps a flood bounded (3 a second, sustained) while a host editing every control twice in 10 s stays inside it.
- A budget refusal (local count or Redis verdict) of a `host:settings` sent with an ack is answered by that ack `{ ok: false, error: "rate-limited" }` instead of `rejected`: the form shows its existing "did not answer, try again" (`timeout`) line at once instead of after 5 s. No new copy.
- The answered refusal still counts toward `REFUSALS_TO_DISCONNECT` (3 in 10 s): the cut rule keeps protecting the server. A human on the form would need 33 commits in 10 s to be cut.
- New ack enum value = wire change: `PROTOCOL_VERSION` 7 -> 8; `INTERNAL_HMAC_TEST_VECTOR` body and signature recomputed with Node `crypto` (the old signature was reproduced first).
- Unchanged: payload-cap refusals (still `rejected`), other events' limits, keys, subjects, socket cap, fail-closed.

## Gotchas
- Socket.IO's packet middleware sees the ack callback as the packet's last element; the guard calls it directly.
- `socket.test.ts` (#656 C2) passes an explicit `version: 7` to `connectToRoom`; it is not tied to `PROTOCOL_VERSION`.
