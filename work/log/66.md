# #66 Settings screen: shell, preferences persistence, layout, theme, language, sound

Date: 2026-11-09. Outcome: built (UI builder); awaiting Deliver.

## Decisions
- No ADR. Follows 0001, 0009, 0010. Bible 14.3 gains the "Settings" extension (form-screen family, `FORM 1-F`, segmented radio groups, layout cards with Plex Mono key caps, FILING… / FILED save state, account rows).
- `proxy.ts` lets `/settings` through with a guest cookie (session-or-guest, as the card's Context says); a visitor with no cookie still goes to `/sign-in` (#36 C15/C16 unchanged). A stale guest cookie reads defaults and the first save files a new guest (`ensureGuest`).
- Theme `SYSTEM` deletes the `theme` cookie (the header's `setTheme("system")` convention). After sign-in (credentials action and the OAuth hook) `refreshPreferenceCookies` writes the account row's theme/locale over the browser's; no row keeps the cookies.
- `/design` specimens save through a no-op server action (`src/app/design/settings-demo-action.ts`).

## Gotchas
- The radios are visually hidden inside their labels: e2e click the label (`choose()` in `e2e/identity/settings-helpers.ts`), and find a section by its chosen radio, since a language change renames the section.
